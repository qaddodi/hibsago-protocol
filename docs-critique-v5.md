# HIBSAGO protocol page: critique of version 5

I reviewed the published page at laptop width (1400 px) and phone width (390 px) against the October 2026 PI.

## Logic errors (fix first)

1. **The order sequence is backwards.** The week 12 decision box asks "What is the qHBsAg?", and the order for that same test sits beside it. The reader decides before the page tells them to draw the lab. Week 48 has the same problem with four labs. Every decision needs to read: draw at this visit → wait for the result → follow the branch.
2. **The "≥1 IU/mL" branch at week 12 goes nowhere.** It says "consider stopping" and ends. Nothing tells the clinician what happens next: NrtI, post-stop rechecks, or when to look at stopping NrtI. Only the "continue" branch has an arrow.
3. **The post-stop rechecks are in the wrong place.** They sit next to week 24, but they actually happen during weeks 25–48. Meanwhile the weeks 25–48 box says "only the rechecks above", which sends the reader back up the page.
4. **The week 48 timing hides an assumption.** The PI ties NrtI-stop eligibility to "24 weeks of further NrtI after completing 24 weeks of HIBSAGO." The page doesn't say what applies if HIBSAGO was stopped early.
5. **"Often ≥1 yr" is invented precision.** The PI says "frequent monitoring" for at least a year and gives no interval. The page should say exactly that.

## Hierarchy and layout

6. **The hold/stop rules are cut off from treatment.** They are the most-used reference during weeks 1–24, yet they sit below the whole algorithm. Nothing in the treatment boxes says "check each result against these rules".
7. **The right-hand "Order" column repeats on every step.** That makes every row look equally important. Standing orders are really one schedule with a single change (LFTs drop to every 2 weeks at week 13). That schedule should be shown once.
8. **The red "Week 12" and "Week 48" tags in the order column** restate the week label. They add red noise and look like warnings.
9. **The decision connectors are a little off-center,** and the arrow out of a split only leaves one branch. The flow looks unfinished.
10. **Green means different things.** At week 12, green means "keep treating". At week 48, green means "stop NrtI". A glance at the color misleads.
11. **The panel key under the title** (CBC = platelets …) is easy to miss. It also repeats inside the hold/stop table.

## Eligibility box

12. **"Must have" and "must not have" are mixed into one checklist,** so the reader has to flip the logic halfway down. They should be split.
13. **The parentheticals crowd the key numbers.** "trials required <90 IU/mL; PI gives no number" is accurate but long. A short footnote would do.
14. **The baseline orders sit in a separate column** from the eligibility values they check (platelets ≥100 comes from the CBC). They should be paired.

## Wording

15. "Twice-weekly doses 3–4 days apart" and "First dose in clinic" are buried in gray subtext. Both are things staff get wrong.
16. "Routine HBV care" is vague, and "watch for flares" doesn't say what to watch.

## What to keep

- The top-to-bottom algorithm with real two-way splits.
- Spelled-out thresholds.
- Labs grouped as CBC / LFTs / BMP.
- A single red reserved for stop and decision.
- The phone layout as one vertical flow.
