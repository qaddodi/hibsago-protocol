HIBSAGO (bepirovirsen) protocol

Added to NrtI for chronic hep B. 24 weeks of HIBSAGO, then at least 24 more weeks of NrtI, then decide on NrtI.

Before day 1
Is the patient eligible?

Must have all

  • Adult, chronic hep B
  • On NrtI ≥6 months
  • HBV DNA suppressed* (trials: <90 IU/mL)
  • qHBsAg ≤3,000 IU/mL
  • Platelets ≥100 ×10⁹/L

Must have none

  • Cirrhosis (any Child-Pugh)
  • Acute hepatic impairment
  • HDV coinfection
  • Peginterferon

Hold / stop rules

Apply to every lab in weeks 1–24.

CBC platelets ×10⁹/L

Hold50–99Restart 300 mg at ≥100. If 50–74, recheck every 2–3 days.
Stop<50Consider steroids with an expert
RecheckFell ≥30% since last count → repeat before dosing

LFTs ALT, total bilirubin

WatchALT 10–12× ULNKeep dosing. LFTs twice a week until ALT falls.
HoldALT ≥12× ULN, or ALT ≥3× + bili 1.5–2× ULNLFTs twice a week. Restart 150 mg when ALT <10× (and bili <1.5×); back to 300 mg as ALT keeps falling.
StopALT ≥10× for 4+ weeks, or ALT ≥3× + bili ≥2× ULN

Also

  • Missed dose: give it as soon as possible. If the next dose is due in under 3 days, skip it.
  • Rotate injection sites every week (thigh or belly). Omental infarction was reported when sites weren't rotated.
  • Caution: anticoagulants or bleeding risk, eGFR ≤60, autoimmune disease or vasculitis, transplant.
  • HIV coinfection: only the HIV provider stops the NrtI.
