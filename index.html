HIBSAGO (bepirovirsen) protocol

Added to NrtI for chronic hep B. 24 weeks of HIBSAGO, then at least 24 more weeks of NrtI, then decide on NrtI.

Before day 1
Is the patient eligible?

Must have all

  • Adult, chronic hep B
  • On NrtI ≥6 months
  • HBV DNA suppressed* (trials: <90 IU/mL)
  • qHBsAg ≤3,000 IU/mL
  • Platelets ≥100 ×10⁹/L

Must have none

  • Cirrhosis (any Child-Pugh)
  • Acute hepatic impairment
  • HDV coinfection
  • Peginterferon

Also

  • Missed dose: give it as soon as possible. If the next dose is due in under 3 days, skip it.
  • Rotate injection sites every week (thigh or belly). Omental infarction was reported when sites weren't rotated.
  • Caution: anticoagulants or bleeding risk, eGFR ≤60 or urine ACR >0.3 mg/mg (excluded from trials), autoimmune disease or vasculitis, transplant.
  • HIV coinfection: only the HIV provider stops the NrtI.
